📊 Retail Profitability Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview
An interactive retail profitability dashboard developed to analyze sales, profit, discounts, customers, products, regions, and shipping performance from 2023–2026. The dashboard helps identify profitable categories, loss-making orders, high-impact discounts, and areas requiring business improvement.

💡 Key Findings
Technology generated the highest profit margin.
Furniture generated high sales but comparatively lower profit.
Discounts above 30% significantly reduced profitability.
Tables and Bookcases were identified as major loss-making areas.
Central Region required further investigation due to weaker profitability.
Technology and Home Office showed stronger business performance.

🎯 Business Recommendations
Reduce excessive discounts, especially above 30%.
Review pricing and profitability of Furniture products.
Investigate loss-making sub-categories.
Improve performance in weaker regions.
Focus investment on high-performing Technology products.
Promote profitable customer segments and shipping methods.
